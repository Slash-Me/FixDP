Fix Docking Port A small plugin for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x that fixes stuck docking ports. 
It adds a Force Undock command that separates two docked vessels even when the stock Undock button is missing or does nothing. 
The problem Sometimes, when you try to undock, the two ports of a docked pair end up in inconsistent states: one port loses its Undock button (and/or its normal docking node),
or both ports claim to be undocked while the vessels are still physically joined, or the port that still has an Undock button does nothing when you press it. 
Without a fix, the vessels stay docked forever unless you destroy one of the ports.
